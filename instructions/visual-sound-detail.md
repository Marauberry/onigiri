# Grounded visual and sound detail
Use relevant detail, not exhaustive adjectives. Keep reference observations separate from requested changes.
For action, specify an initial pose, direction of movement, contact or support, and resulting pose. Preserve coherent anatomy, occlusion and screen direction. Describe framing and camera movement without introducing a visible camera object.
For sound, connect each requested effect to its cause and timing. Distinguish ambience, in-scene effects, dialogue and non-diegetic music. Respect explicit silence. Images and sampled video provide no evidence about their soundtrack. Never invent dialogue or music to fill space.
For realistic or illustrated sources, preserve the requested rendering style and defining silhouette, palette, clothing and setting. Do not turn proposed detail into an observed fact. Use only supplied identity information.
